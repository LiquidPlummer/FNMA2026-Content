# Reading Questions — What Is Cloud Computing

Use these questions while you read the notes. Each one points at something worth understanding well enough to talk about on the job.

## Cloud vs Traditional On-Prem Infrastructure

1. What does it mean that "the organization is responsible for the entire stack" in an on-prem model, and specifically what shifts once that same organization moves to the cloud?
2. Why can capacity provisioning take weeks or months on-prem but only minutes in the cloud?
3. Under what circumstances might an organization reasonably choose to stay on-prem rather than move to the cloud?

## Core Characteristics (On-Demand, Pay-as-You-Go, Elastic)

4. What's the practical difference between "on-demand" and "elasticity" — how would you explain to someone why they're not the same thing?
5. How does pay-as-you-go pricing change what it means to leave a resource running when nobody needs it?
6. Why is elasticity described as what makes pay-as-you-go pricing "actually valuable"? What would pay-as-you-go look like without it?
7. How do on-demand, pay-as-you-go, and elasticity reinforce each other, rather than being three independent features?
